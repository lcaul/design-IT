User Guide Usage Information FAQs

How to Use This Website

This website helps users understand their household water usage and learn simple ways to save water.

  1. Log in or create an account.
  2. Go to the dashboard or homepage.
  3. Check your household water usage information.
  4. View your home details, such as household size and basic information.
  5. Read water-saving tips if your water usage is high.
  6. Use this FAQ page if you need help.

Usage Information

Water usage information shows how much water a household uses. Users can check their daily or weekly water usage on the dashboard. This helps users notice if their water usage suddenly becomes higher than normal.

By checking this information, users can better understand their water habits and make small changes, such as taking shorter showers, fixing leaking taps, and using water-saving appliances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is this website used for?

This website is used to help households track water usage and learn simple ways to save water.

How can I check my water usage?

After logging in, users can go to the dashboard to view their household water usage information.

What should I do if my water usage is too high?

You can check the water-saving tips and try to reduce unnecessary water use at home.

Can I update my household information?

Yes. Users can go to the home details page to update basic household information.

Why is saving water important?

Saving water can reduce water bills, protect the environment, and support long-term water sustainability.
