User Guide & FAQs

Learn how to use WaterWise and understand your household water usage more easily.

How to Use WaterWise

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the dashboard to check water usage information.
  3. View household details and update information if needed.
  4. Track daily or weekly water usage patterns.
  5. Read water-saving tips and recommendations.

Understanding Water Usage

WaterWise helps users better understand how much water their household uses. Tracking usage regularly can help identify high water consumption and encourage better water-saving habits.

Small changes such as shorter showers, fixing leaks, and reducing unnecessary water use can make a big difference over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WaterWise?

WaterWise is a simple platform that helps households track water usage and learn ways to save water.

How can I check my water usage?

You can check your estimated water usage through the dashboard page.

Why is saving water important?

Saving water helps reduce water bills and supports environmental sustainability.

Can I update household information?

Yes. Household details can be updated through the Household Details page.
